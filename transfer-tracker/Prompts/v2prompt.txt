Important Business Rules
Work Week

The transfer week is:

Wednesday → Tuesday

NOT Monday-Sunday.

All weekly calculations must use this.

Bonus Rules

Bonus thresholds are DAILY, not weekly.

Important:

You must reach the threshold each individual day.

Example:

Wednesday:
2 approved transfers

No approved bonus yet
Thursday:
3 approved transfers

Approved bonus activates for Thursday only

Do NOT calculate threshold weekly.

Bonus Placeholder Rules
