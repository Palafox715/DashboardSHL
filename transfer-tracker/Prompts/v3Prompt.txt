# Transfer Tracker Project — Continuation Prompt

I am building a personal call center transfer tracking web app.

The goal is a fast, lightweight productivity tool for tracking approved and declined call transfers and estimating bonus pay.

## Tech Stack

* HTML5
* CSS3
* Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
* IndexedDB only
* No frameworks
* No backend
* No login

The app should prioritize:

* Speed
* Keyboard-first workflow
* Minimal clicks
* Real-time tracking
* Mobile-friendly (400px width)
* Dark mode by default

---

# Current Workflow

Current workflow is:

Paste customer information

↓

Paste phone number

↓

Click **Approved** or **Declined**

↓

Transfer saves instantly

↓

Inputs clear automatically

↓

Ready for next call

Target entry time is **2–3 seconds per transfer**.

# Business Rules

Work week is:

Wednesday → Tuesday

NOT Monday–Sunday.

All weekly calculations must use Wednesday as the beginning of the week.

---

# Bonus Rules

Bonus is DAILY.

Thresholds do NOT accumulate across the week.

Example:

Wednesday:

2 Approved

↓

No approved bonus

Thursday:

3 Approved

↓

Approved bonus activates only for Thursday.

Weekly bonus is simply the sum of each day's earned bonus.
